## What internet speed is needed for IPTV on Xiaomi Mi Box?

For a Xiaomi Mi Box IPTV setup, internet stability matters almost as much as the subscription.

TiviLive recommends at least **16 Mbps for HD** and **25+ Mbps for 4K/UHD**. It also says Ethernet is preferred for stability, although a strong Wi-Fi connection can be used.

In everyday use, that means a few things are worth checking before blaming the IPTV app:

* Is the Mi Box receiving a strong Wi-Fi signal?
* Are other devices using a large amount of bandwidth at the same time?
* Does the stream buffer only at peak times or all the time?
* Does lowering the stream quality make playback smoother?

The provider itself recommends switching to Ethernet, restarting the router, closing bandwidth-heavy apps or lowering stream quality when buffering occurs.
